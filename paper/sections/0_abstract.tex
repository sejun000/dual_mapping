\begin{abstract}

High-capacity QLC SSDs cannot keep a 4\,KB page-level mapping table in
on-device DRAM. Under a limited DRAM budget, existing FTLs slow reads with
mapping misses, slow writes with block-level merges, or turn small writes into
read-modify-writes of a uniform coarse indirection unit (IU). We present DUET,
an FTL that keeps the coarse IU and maps smaller writes as 4\,KB pages in a DRAM
hash table. DUET spills these entries to NAND when the table fills and merges
fine pages one coarse IU at a time. With 1/8 of the page-level mapping DRAM,
DUET reaches 94\% of the throughput of a page-level FTL with unlimited DRAM,
whereas existing FTLs reach at most 73\%.
\end{abstract}
