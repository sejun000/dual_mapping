\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
DUET maps most data with coarse IUs and small writes with 4\,KB fine pages, evicts
fine-map entries to NAND when its DRAM table fills, and merges fine pages one coarse
IU at a time. With about 1/8 of the page-level mapping DRAM, DUET reaches 91\% of the
throughput of a page-level FTL with unlimited DRAM.
